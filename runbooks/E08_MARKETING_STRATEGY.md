# E08 Marketing Strategy Runbook

1. Require a project-scoped Approved Story exact ref; draft/latest Story is forbidden.
2. Resolve approved profiles; equal-priority hard conflicts block generation.
3. Enforce CandidateBudget before provider calls and preserve unavailable/incomplete outcomes.
4. Verify every Selling Point and Hook source against Story Event/Evidence; never invent drama facts.
5. Inspect critic disagreement, blockers, diversity, feasibility and cost independently.
6. Gate 2 must select only refs inside the exact Strategy Review Package; stale decisions fail closed.
7. Stage 4 reads only approved Creative Brief and Variant Plan pointers.
8. Treat variants as candidates until controlled exposure and outcome lineage exist.
9. On Story/config/profile change, invalidate Strategy and downstream exact dependency closure.
10. Keep IDs in traces, not metric labels; alert on blocker attempts, stale decisions and budget rejects.
