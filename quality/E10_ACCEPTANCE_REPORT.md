# E10 Voice, Audio and Subtitle Engineering Acceptance Report

Version: 1.0  
Date: 2026-08-12  
Decision: Superseded by ADR-047; advance contract baseline only, E10 not started.

E10 implements provider-neutral bounded Voice Takes, selected Voice Asset and actual duration,
Alignment, conform delta, rights-bearing audio selection, explicit narration/BGM ducking and loudness
intent, non-overlapping safe-area Subtitle Cues and ASS artifact boundary.

Three engineering checks pass. TTS/voice license and benchmark, real BGM/SFX/font rights, full-program
sync/loudness/subtitle QC and production audio concurrency remain blockers. No synthetic tone or typed
contract is treated as proof of expressive voice, intelligibility or final mix quality.
